怎么玩

How it plays

  1. 拍一拍

    任意键都行，小手乱拍也完全没问题——小动物立刻从草丛里蹦出来。

  2. Tap

    Any key works, wild banging included. An animal pops out of the grass right away.

  3. 有回应

    每个小动物有名字、有声音、有大有小；拍得多了，它们还会排成一队出发。

  4. The world answers

    Every creature has a name, a sound, a size. Keep tapping and they line up and parade off together.

  5. 你来说

    「你看，兔子！」屏幕只负责「有回应」这个事实，意义由爸爸妈妈来给。

  6. You narrate

    "Look, a rabbit!" The screen only promises that the world answers. The meaning comes from you.

给家长

For parents

  • 完全离线：不联网、无广告、无内购，不收集任何数据。
  • Fully offline: no network, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no data collection.
  • 家长门：设置要长按 P 两秒才打得开，小手乱拍进不去。
  • Parent gate: settings open only after holding P for two seconds, safe from little hands.
  • 幼儿锁定：全屏运行防误触，长按 Esc 才会退出。
  • Toddler lock: runs full screen; only a long hold on Esc quits.
  • 语音报名字：系统合成语音，默认关闭——开不开，你说了算。
  • Spoken names: uses the built-in macOS voice and is off by default. You decide.
  • 七种语言：中文、英语、日语、韩语、西班牙语、法语、德语。
  • Seven languages: Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, German.
  • 源码公开：MIT 协议，每一行代码都可以检查。
  • Open source: MIT licensed, every line yours to inspect.

为什么会有这个 App

Why this app exists

这个 App 是一位爸爸为 15 个月大的女儿写的。她特别喜欢拍键盘——这个年龄的乱砸本身就是认真做事：她在用自己仅有的手段，一遍遍验证「我的动作会让世界有回应」。

This app was written by a dad for his 15-month-old daughter. She loves banging on the keyboard — at this age, banging is serious work: with the only means she has, she tests over and over that her actions make the world respond.

于是有了它：拍一下，小动物就蹦出来，爸爸在旁边说：「你看，兔子！」屏幕只负责「世界有回应」这个事实，意义由你来给——孩子负责拍，你负责说。

So he wrote this: tap, and an animal pops up — "Look, a rabbit!" The app offers the fact that the world answers; the meaning comes from you. The child taps, you talk.
