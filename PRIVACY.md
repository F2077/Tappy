# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

**English**

Pat-a-Pet works entirely offline. It collects, transmits, and stores no
user data, and contains no ads, analytics, or third-party tracking. All
settings stay on your device.

Questions or issues: please open an issue on
[GitHub](https://github.com/F2077/Tappy/issues).

**中文**

拍拍小世界（Pat-a-Pet）完全离线运行，不收集、不传输、不存储任何
用户数据，不含广告、分析工具或第三方追踪。所有设置仅保存在本机。

如有问题或建议，请在 [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/F2077/Tappy/issues)
反馈。
